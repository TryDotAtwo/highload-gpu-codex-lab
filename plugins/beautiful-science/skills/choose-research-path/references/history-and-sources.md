# Historical counterweights and source limits

Historical cases constrain a slogan; they do not measure a universal probability that beautiful ideas are true. A later successful descendant does not retroactively validate its ancestor, and a later attractive presentation does not establish the original motive for discovery.

## Beautiful expectations that needed correction

### Weyl: a structure can survive a failed physical interpretation

The 1918 geometry concerned local scale/length; Einstein's measurement objection and Weyl's reply concern its connection to physical standards. Weyl's 1929 construction involves quantum phase. The formal kinship is fruitful, but the physical interpretation changed. Retaining the organizing idea does not prove the original length interpretation was right.

**Decision lesson:** separate original theory, reusable structure, and the overall research investment. A change from physical application to mathematical investigation must be named.

Sources: [Weyl 1918, English translation, addendum and reply](https://www.neo-classical-physics.info/uploads/3/4/3/6/34363841/weyl_-_grav._and_electr.pdf), [Weyl 1929, introduction and §6](https://www.neo-classical-physics.info/uploads/3/4/3/6/34363841/weyl_-_electron_and_gravitation_i.pdf). The supplied manuscript inspected these translated versions. This package does not infer that Weyl dismissed experiment or attribute an unverified aphorism to him.

### Parity: successful symmetry has an empirical boundary

Lee and Yang's 1956 analysis distinguished evidence across interaction classes and proposed tests. Wu and collaborators' 1957 cobalt-60 experiment reported a controlled beta-decay asymmetry. This restricts the relevant parity claim; it does not reject symmetry methods generally or prove that all physicists were motivated by aesthetic prejudice.

**Decision lesson:** require evidence for the transfer into a new regime. A controlled contradiction outranks the expectation that a more symmetric law ought to hold.

Sources: [Lee and Yang, 1956, Physical Review 104, 254](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.104.254), [Wu et al., 1957, Physical Review 105, 1413](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.105.1413). The manuscript inspected the primary articles; source support concerns the argument and observations, not a universal psychological story.

## Successful complexity without compulsory admiration

### Four colors: the proof's obligations matter more than brevity

Gonthier's 2008 account of the formal four-color theorem explains that verifying a program alone is insufficient: its specification must connect to the mathematical theorem. Large case verification can coexist with a comprehensible reduction architecture.

**Decision lesson:** retain a difficult, checkable path when it closes the actual obligations. A shorter outline with missing arrows is not an improvement in certainty. One may value the result while still wanting a more explanatory proof.

Source: [Gonthier, Formal Proof—The Four-Color Theorem, 2008, pp. 1382–1393](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/JavaAndDS/files/Gonthier4ColorCoq.pdf). The manuscript inspected this university-hosted copy. This is not a claim that the proof is objectively ugly or that computational checks are immune to specification errors.

### Kepler: formalization has interfaces to audit

The Flyspeck report describes a large formal proof using HOL Light and Isabelle. The 2015 author preprint discusses the graph-classification interface and manually matched formulations. Two systems do not automatically amount to two independent complete proofs of the same implementation.

**Decision lesson:** count trust boundaries and exact theorem statements. Large verification effort can be substantive progress even when it is not visually simple.

Sources: [Hales et al., 2015 author preprint, sections 8 and 10](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.02155), [published 2017 report](https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2017.1). Detailed interface claims here follow the preprint used in the manuscript; the versions are not silently treated as identical.

## Provenance and what this plugin does not establish

This protocol adapts the supplied Russian manuscript *Научная красота: Формы понимания, эвристики открытия и пределы эстетического суждения*, discussion version dated 1 October 2026, especially its comparative synthesis and research-practice chapters. Related chapters on computation, proof, symmetry, empirical methods, and historical selection constrain the examples above. Source hashes are in the package's `reports/source-provenance.json`; the manuscript itself is not required at runtime.

The plugin author inspected the supplied manuscript and local chapter/ledger texts. External-source access descriptions above preserve the manuscript's recorded scope; they are not claims of a new full primary-literature audit during plugin packaging. No quotation is used as authority for the protocol.

The rules are normative research practice. Development tests check a small set of agent decisions. Neither the book nor this package establishes universal effectiveness, calibrated discovery probabilities, or a cross-domain aesthetic ranking.
