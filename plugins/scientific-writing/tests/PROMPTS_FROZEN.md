## E1 — English explanatory prose

Write a self-contained English passage for an interested secondary-school reader, no more than 220 words. Preserve every condition, number, calculation, and limitation, but choose a clear explanatory order. Do not provide an editorial checklist. Source: We tested ten seeds from one batch on damp paper at 20 °C. By day five, seven had germinated. Thus 7/10 = 70%. This observation describes that batch under these conditions, not the germination rate of every seed of this species. Germination means the seed starts growing, not that it has grown into a mature plant. A second group was kept cooler, but no observations or counts from it are available. The tempting conclusion that warmth caused the 70% result cannot be drawn from these data. Tell the reader what we observed and what it lets us conclude without turning the passage into a report about your compliance with instructions.
