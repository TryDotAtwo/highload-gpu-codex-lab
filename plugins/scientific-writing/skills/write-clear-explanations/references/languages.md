# Language routing and evidence boundary

The reasoning and preservation principles apply across languages. Idiom, word order, agreement, punctuation, register, and rhythm require the norms of the actual target language. Preserve that language unless translation is requested. In mixed-language documents, check each passage in its own language. Do not translate Russian sentence patterns or English style prohibitions into universal rules.

## Russian

Read [Russian techniques](ru/techniques.md) for idiom/rhythm and the historical editorial inspiration, [Russian examples](ru/examples.md) when comparing revisions, and [reading ledger](ru/reading.md) for source scope. These are inherited Russian materials. Their literary examples are Russian, not templates for every language. The companion fidelity reference is retained as historical Russian guidance.

## English

Use English-specific instructions in technical-style-editor when appropriate. Keep explicit subjects, intelligible reference chains, and conditions beside claims without imposing identical sentence lengths. The following is a newly authored illustration, not an experiment or evidence of reader understanding:

“Ten seeds from one batch were placed on damp paper at 20 °C. By day five, seven had begun to grow: 7/10, or 70%. That percentage describes this batch under these conditions; it does not tell us the germination rate of every seed of the species.”

This demonstrates an observed result with local conditions and a limited inference. It is not the response to a completed behavioral test.

## Other languages

Apply the common reasoning checks and the language's own conventions. Use an authoritative language reference or qualified review when a consequential language-specific point is uncertain. Do not advertise EN/RU lint rules as validators for another language.

## Validation claims

The inherited tests concern particular Russian passages and revisions only. They establish neither superiority in English or other languages nor robust performance for all genres. New English and Prism scenarios are separately recorded as NOT_RUN unless actual response evidence is present. Author review, structural validation, successful compilation, factual correctness, and empirical comprehension are distinct checks.
