# Write clear explanations

Let readers reconstruct the reasoning. Supply the relevant detail, connections, and examples without deleting technical depth. Audience knowledge determines the support needed, not which true claims may be discarded.

## Scope and preparation

For preparation or substantial editing of an article, book, report, or LaTeX manuscript, **also use work-with-prism**. Its real project-and-assistant workflow is required; reading local instructions does not satisfy it. Ordinary short answers, sentence-level edits, and spelling corrections do not trigger that workflow by themselves.

Establish purpose, audience, known background, requested language, and editing boundaries from the request. Ask only about missing information that changes the work. For revision, map the claims to preserve: definitions, facts, formulas, examples, quantities, hypotheses, domains, distinctions, and unresolved questions. Keep this editorial map outside the reader-facing prose.

Read [meaning fidelity](references/fidelity.md) for substantive revision. Read [language routing](references/languages.md) before applying language-specific norms. For scientific claims and figures also use write-visual-research-papers; for English/Russian sentence-level cleanup use technical-style-editor. These roles complement one another; style review does not establish scientific truth.

## Build the explanation

For substantial revision, rebuild the explanation from preserved meanings: what question drives each episode, and which result does the next need? Content preservation does not require preserving paragraph order. For a narrow edit, keep the requested boundary.

Introduce the object before acting on it, the condition before the conclusion, and a symbol when first used. Show the transition a reader would otherwise have to guess: what changes, what stays fixed, and why the next step follows. Keep formulas and explain the meaningful operations beside them. A worked case prepares a general argument; it does not replace proof.

Each paragraph advances understanding using an object or result already established. Retain a stable term when synonyms or pronouns would hide its identity. A new example should test a boundary, reveal an exception, or prepare a generalization. The conclusion answers the actual question.

## Write for the reader

Give actions concrete subjects. Vary sentence structure with the thought rather than enforcing a word count, dialogue, or question quota. Preserve natural syntax in the requested language. Put conditions where their claims operate. Distinguish proposed actions and expected outcomes from observations that actually occurred. Once a necessary limitation is clear, advance the explanation rather than repeating the compliance checklist.

## Read and verify

Read the complete passage without the editorial checklist first. Can the reader follow the subject and purpose of each step? Then compare meanings, formulas, conditions, references, and figures against the source. After reordering episodes, verify their inherited conditions. Repetition needs a new job: preventing ambiguity, carrying a needed premise, or changing the conclusion.

Report substantive source errors separately and propose a checkable correction. Deliver the requested prose, with a brief editorial note only when useful. Checklist completion does not establish reader comprehension; keep actual reader feedback distinct from editorial judgment.
